## Time-use data
### Statbel Time Use Survey (TUS) — **confidence: low-medium (not opened)**
- Statbel runs a time-use survey with a one-week diary; participation is voluntary; the TOR research group of VUB (and spin-off hbits) has worked with Statbel on it since 1999 [S]. Page: https://statbel.fgov.be/en/survey/time-use-survey-tus (bot-protected for my fetcher). A "2022" Statbel TUS result set was **not found**; I cannot confirm that a 2022 wave was published or what its tables contain.
- Breakdown by income group, education, region (Brussels): **not found**. Microdata would need an application through Statbel / VUB-TOR.
- Harmonised alternative: Multinational Time Use Study (MTUS) lists Belgian surveys at https://www.timeuse.org/mtus/surveys/BEL [S] (registration; not opened). Eurostat HETUS (Harmonised European Time Use Surveys) has Belgium 2013 wave — not opened. Belgian Eurostat TUS report leaflet at https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/documents/9986036/10970806/847218_BE_final+report_Leaflet.pdf [S].

### Working hours (Eurostat LFS, via Brussels Times articles) — **confidence: medium**
- Average usual weekly hours in Belgium in 2025: 34.3 (employees 35.2; self-employed without employees 43.2, the largest gap between employees and own-account workers in the EU) [S] https://brusselstimes.com/belgium/2188384/clocking-on-how-long-is-belgiums-working-week-compared-with-other-eu-countries/. Eurostat series for the underlying data: lfsa_ewhun2 (hours by sex, age, status, activity) [S]. I did not pull the dataset itself.
- An older Brussels Times item reports full-time self-employed Belgians averaging 59.6 hours a week [S] https://www.brusselstimes.com/employment/56176/self-employed-belgians-outwork-fellow-europeans/ — dated and measure unverified.
- One in six independents earned under €12,500 (2016 data, older Brussels Times report) [S].
- Evidence of long hours at **both ends of the income scale**: only an indirect signal — self-employed workers have the longest hours and (per the 2016 item) many have very low incomes — **no study found** showing hours by income decile, nor second-job holding by income. For high earners, no Belgian source found. EU-SILC and LFS microdata would be needed (Eurostat access via application).
- Part-time share 2022: 25.6% of active population (Statista citing Statbel) [S] — not verified at source.
