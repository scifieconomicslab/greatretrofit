# Audit: how much of plan §4 does `retrofit_v3` implement?

Audit date: 2026-11-06, against `main` at commit `1719a88` (the skeleton, before the work this branch adds). Method: read `docs/plans/2026-11-06-interactive-retrofit-plan.md` §4 line by line, then `v3/src/retrofit_v3/*.py` and `packs/great-retrofit/runtime.js`, and checked each mechanism against what the code does, not what its docstrings say. Verdicts: **yes** (implemented as the plan describes), **partial** (a usable piece exists, something the plan asks for is missing), **no** (absent). Column "After" records what this branch did about it (see `model-spec.md` for the resulting rules).

The skeleton's test suite (seed reproducibility, conservation, rebate bounds, monotonicity, zero-savings, capacity) passes on `main`. Passing says the *implemented* parts are consistent; it says nothing about the missing ones.

## Mechanism by mechanism

| # | Plan §4 mechanism | Verdict on `main` | Where, and what is missing | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Owner-occupied rule: act if NPV over the household's horizon is positive **and** the cost can be financed (savings, or credit if granted) | **yes** | `model.run_simulation` (candidate loop) calls `financing.plan_financing`, then `npv.npv_retrofit` over `u.horizon - t` months; `ownership.OwnerOccupied` is a flat monthly consideration chance. NPV terms: PV savings + PV rebate − cash from savings − PV loan payments. The plan's formula also has a "financing cost" term: it is there only implicitly, as the PV of loan payments. | unchanged (rewritten into `Simulation`) |
| 2 | Condo unit rule: each owner decides for their unit | **yes** | `ownership.Condo`, same loop. | unchanged |
| 3 | Condo: a neighbour's retrofit raises the chance of following | **partial** | `ownership.Condo.consider_probability` = `base + neighbour_boost * adopted_share_in_building`. Works, but there is no switch to turn the effect off cleanly for a with/without comparison (setting the boost to 0 is the only way), and "neighbour" means same building only. | `peer_effect` toggle added; scope (same building) documented as an assumption |

| 6 | Credit access (a lender who approves or refuses, fixed or household-dependent rate) | **partial** | `credit.credit_limit` implements three access rules (`income_tested`, `open`, `none`) and a loan-to-income cap; one rate for everyone (`loan_rate`). Two gaps: (i) it does not apply to the rebate; (ii) an ineligible household simply cannot retrofit at all, rather than buying on the market without subsidy, which overstates what a means test excludes. | applies to all modes; ineligible households buy at market price with no subsidy |

## Side findings

- **Parity coverage.** `cases.json` on `main` had ten cases covering every mode and credit rule, refundable and means test, but no case could exercise rows 4, 5 or 3 (off switch), because those mechanisms did not exist. Condo buildings appeared only in the two explicit-building cases.
- **Test blind spots.** There was no invariant test for peer effects, for cost decomposition, or for time budgets: none could be written without the mechanism.
- **Docs.** `placeholders.md` already listed rows 4 and 5 as "not implemented", consistently with this audit. The frame schema documented 8 series; it now documents the additions.
- **Plan wording.** The Phase 1 test list in the plan stated the rebate cap twice ("rebate never exceeds cost or tax capacity" and "rebate never exceeds cost or tax payable"). Fixed in this branch.
